Reflection
The dataset shows how the recorded prices of four items changed across two cities between January and March 2026. All four average prices were higher on the last date, and City 2 was PKR 6 per kg higher than City 1 in each matching comparison. However, the data cannot explain why prices changed or whether people could afford the items. It does not include income, wages or transport costs. The file is also labelled as illustrative teaching data, not actual PBS observations, so the results should only be used to describe this dataset.
Unsupported suggestion
The most tempting extra question was whether rising prices reduced household purchasing power. This is an important issue, but the dataset does not include household income, wages or spending. For that reason, the question cannot be answered using this data.
