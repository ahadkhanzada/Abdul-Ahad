Initial claims and questions
Claim 1
Prices of everyday items have increased over time.
Question: How did the average price of each item change between 1 January 2026 and 19 March 2026?
Variables: date, item, city and price.
Claim 2
The same item can have different prices in different cities.
Question: Was the price in City 2 higher than the price in City 1 for the same item on the same date?
Variables: date, item, city and price.
Dataset check
The dataset has 96 rows and 6 columns. It contains four items, two cities and 12 dates. There are no missing values.
The file covers the period from 1 January 2026 to 19 March 2026.
Results
Average prices across both cities:
Item	First date	Last date	Change
Oil B	301.00	347.00	+46.00
Pulse C	203.00	210.50	+7.50
Staple A	104.00	117.50	+13.50
Sweetener D	152.00	181.50	+29.50
All four average prices were higher on the last date. Sweetener D had the largest percentage increase, about 19.41%.
City 2 was PKR 6 per kg more expensive than City 1 for each matching item and date.
Limitation
The dataset is labelled as illustrative teaching data, not actual PBS observations. It cannot show why prices changed or measure household affordability.
